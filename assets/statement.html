• Safety Notice

  1. This product is not suitable for persons under 18 years of age and others who do not have full civil capacity.
  2. This product has a large body size, high-speed rotating propellers, and powerful flight power, and has certain dangers during operation. Improper operation and use of this product may cause danger and injury.
  3. When using this product, please stay away from dangerous environments such as airports, railways, highways, high-rise buildings, and power lines.
  4. When using this product, please stay away from high electromagnetic interference environments such as mobile phone base stations and high-power transmitting equipment.
  5. When using this product, please stay away from various manned aircraft.
  6. Please do not use this product in harsh environments such as rain, lightning, sandstorms, fog, snow, strong winds, and low temperatures.
  7. When flying above 3000 meters above sea level, environmental factors will cause flight performance degradation. Please use this product with caution.
  8. This product is not waterproof. Please do not attempt to land on water.
  9. When operating this product at low altitude, please always maintain a safe distance of more than 10 meters between the drone and people or animals.
  10. When using this product in non-remote areas, please always keep the drone flying within the operator's line of sight.
  11. Do not hover or fly this product over crowds. Do not use it to frighten others for fun.
  12. When there are onlookers approaching, please operate carefully and land as soon as possible to avoid accidents.
  13. Please do not operate this product near children's play areas.
  14. Please do not use this product to chase vehicles or affect the normal operation of vehicles.
  15. Unless absolutely necessary, please do not turn off the motor when this product is flying in the air.
  16. Please do not try to stop any moving parts of this product during operation.
  17. Please do not block the heat dissipation windows/ports.
  18. Please do not modify this product or use it for purposes other than its original design.
  19. This product does not include cargo functions. Please do not use it for transportation purposes.
  20. Please operate according to the product manual.
  21. Please use original accessories for maintenance and replacement.
  22. When lending to other personnel for operation, please ensure that the operators understand and comply with this safety notice.

Refer to your drone user manual for the latest safety guidance.
